Parakeet TDT 0.6B v2 (English speech recognition)
Original model: NVIDIA, https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v2
Model license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
VectorDash optionally downloads the ONNX conversion published by k2-fsa:
https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/tree/master/scripts/nemo/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v2
Inference runtime: sherpa-onnx, Apache-2.0 (with ONNX Runtime, MIT).
https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx
CPU model is quantized int8; NVIDIA CUDA model is fp16.
Optional artifacts are SHA-256 pinned in parakeet-downloads.json.
They are not included in the base installer. No speech is sent to an online service.

Native speech ABI declarations follow the sherpa-onnx 1.13.8 bundled C API header (Copyright 2023 Xiaomi Corporation, Apache-2.0). Full terms are in licenses/Sherpa-ONNX-APACHE.txt.
